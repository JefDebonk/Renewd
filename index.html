Questions

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything you add is stored locally in your own browser (localStorage) — it never gets uploaded to a server, and nothing about your finances or documents is shared with anyone, including us.

What happens if I switch devices or clear my browser?

Use the "Export backup" link in the footer any time to save a JSON file of everything you're tracking, and "Import backup" to bring it into a new browser or device. We'd recommend doing this occasionally either way.

Is Renewd really free?

Yes, the core tracker is free with no account required. A future optional paid tier will add email/SMS reminders, calendar sync, and shared lists for families — but tracking your own deadlines will always stay free.

Why not just use a spreadsheet or my phone's reminders?

You can! Renewd just does the sorting and urgency-highlighting for you automatically, so you see what actually needs attention in one glance instead of scanning a flat list or scattered reminders.
